Sustainability at Alyra

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

At Alyra, sustainability is at the core of everything we do. We are committed to reducing our environmental footprint by using algae-based fabrics that are renewable and biodegradable.

Our CSR initiatives include supporting ocean conservation projects, reducing waste in our production processes, and ensuring fair labor practices throughout our supply chain.

Environmental Impact

By utilizing algae as a raw material, we significantly reduce water usage and carbon emissions compared to traditional textile manufacturing.

We continuously strive to improve our processes and collaborate with partners who share our vision for a healthier planet.

Community Engagement

Alyra actively engages with local communities to promote sustainable practices and education about the benefits of algae-based products.

We believe in transparency and regularly report on our sustainability goals and progress.
