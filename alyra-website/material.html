Algae Fabric & Clothing

Our algae-based fabrics are soft, breathable, and sustainable. They offer a unique texture and feel that is both luxurious and eco-friendly.

Certified for quality and sustainability, our materials meet rigorous standards to ensure durability and environmental responsibility.

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

At Alyra, sustainability is at the core of everything we do. We are committed to reducing our environmental footprint by using algae-based fabrics that are renewable and biodegradable.

Our CSR initiatives include supporting ocean conservation projects, reducing waste in our production processes, and ensuring fair labor practices throughout our supply chain.
